## Rollback

- Worktree removal is reversible while the branch ref lives:
  `git worktree add <path> <branch>`. VM checkouts are
  reversible via reflog. /tmp deletion is NOT reversible by
  design (throwaway; recreatable from the bare repo). If any
  signoff criterion fails, stop, report the failing check
  with its evidence, and do not proceed to signoff.
